# Data access

The analyses use public GEO accessions and derived aggregate tables. Public GEO inputs must be obtained from NCBI GEO under the applicable terms. This upload copy does not redistribute raw GEO matrices, single-cell matrices, OAI/NDA participant-level data, credentials, tokens or private files.

Primary bulk accessions: GSE1919, GSE12021, GSE55235, GSE55457, GSE82107, GSE206848, GSE89408 and GSE143514. External holdout: GSE283079. Single-cell references/context: GSE248453 and GSE283080.

The eight primary cohorts comprise 158 canonical expression-analysis records, not 158 verified independent donors. GSE12021 is restricted to 19 GPL96 profiles (10 OA, 9 controls). GSE283079 and the GSE283080-derived Reference B-v2 belong to the same study family; patient-level independence was not confirmed.

# Data Access

Raw public data should be downloaded from the original public repositories.

- Dataset accession numbers and evidence-layer roles are listed in `dataset_accession_table.csv`.
- OAI and FNIH access must follow the original cohort terms and governance requirements.
- Restricted or raw source data are not redistributed in this repository.
- Only selected derived outputs are included here, and participant-level restricted datasets are not re-shared.
